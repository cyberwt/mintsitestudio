About Mint Site Studio

This site has two parts: Mint GRE, free GRE General Test prep (original practice questions with explanations, a daily question, vocabulary flashcards and study guides), and Mint Puzzles, six free daily puzzles, also available as an iPhone app.

How the GRE practice is made

Every practice question, passage and vocabulary definition on Mint GRE is written by us for this site. None of it is copied from ETS or from commercial prep books. Each question is solved independently a second time before it is published, and we check test facts (timing, question counts, scoring) against ETS's own pages. If you think an answer is wrong, email us and we will re-check it.

About Mint Puzzles

Mint Puzzles publishes six original puzzles every day: three word games (Mint Word, Mint Hive and Mint Box) and three logic games (Mint Sum, Mini Sudoku and Mint Nonogram). They are free, need no account, and work on phones and computers.

How the puzzles are made

Every puzzle is generated by our own code from the date, so everyone sees the same puzzle on the same day. Before a puzzle is shown, a solver checks it:

  • Mint Sum targets are built from the day's numbers, so each round has at least one exact solution.
  • Mini Sudoku clues are removed one at a time, and a clue is only removed if the puzzle still has exactly one solution.
  • Mint Nonogram grids are kept only if they can be solved line by line with no guessing.
  • Mint Word answers are everyday five-letter words, and no answer repeats until the list runs out.
  • Mint Hive letters come from an everyday word that uses all seven, so there is always a pangram, and a day is kept only if it has 20 to 55 answers.
  • Mint Box letters come from two everyday words that chain together and use all twelve, so there is always a two-word answer.

The word games use a word list built from SCOWL (Spell Checker Oriented Word Lists), copyright Kevin Atkinson, used under its permissive license.

Past puzzles stay free to play. Each game page lists the earlier days under "Past puzzles".
