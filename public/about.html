About Mint Puzzles

Mint Puzzles publishes three original puzzles every day: Mint Sum (a number puzzle), Mini Sudoku (6×6) and Mint Nonogram (10×10). They are free, need no account, and work on phones and computers.

How the puzzles are made

Every puzzle is generated by our own code from the date, so everyone sees the same puzzle on the same day. Before a puzzle is shown, a solver checks it:

  • Mint Sum targets are built from the day's numbers, so each round has at least one exact solution.
  • Mini Sudoku clues are removed one at a time, and a clue is only removed if the puzzle still has exactly one solution.
  • Mint Nonogram grids are kept only if they can be solved line by line with no guessing.

Past puzzles stay free to play. Each game page lists the earlier days under "Past puzzles".

Who we are

Mint Puzzles is a small independent project by Mint Site Studio. The site is new (first puzzle: October 21, 2026), and we add games based on what players come back to.
