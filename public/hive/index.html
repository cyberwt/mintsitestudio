Mint Hive

How to play

  • Make words of at least four letters from the seven in the hive. Every word must use the center letter.
  • Letters can be used more than once. Common words only: no names, places or hyphens.
  • Four-letter words score 1 point; longer words score 1 point per letter. Each puzzle has at least one word that uses all seven letters, worth 7 bonus points.
  • Reach the Minty rank (half the points) to solve the day and keep your streak. You can keep going after that.
