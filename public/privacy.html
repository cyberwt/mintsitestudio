Privacy

Last updated November 19, 2026. This page covers the whole site: Mint GRE prep and Mint Puzzles.

  • There are no accounts. We never ask for your name, email or any other personal details.
  • Your practice answers, flashcard progress, puzzle results and streaks are saved in your own browser (localStorage). They never leave your device, and clearing your browser data removes them.
  • We use Google Analytics to count visits and see which pages, practice questions and puzzles people use. It sets cookies and collects data such as pages viewed, time on page, device type and approximate location. See how Google uses this data; you can opt out with the Google Analytics opt-out add-on.
  • Our fonts load from Google Fonts, so your browser sends Google a standard web request (including your IP address) when the page loads.
  • Every page on this website loads Grow, an engagement tool from Mediavine that can offer features such as saving or bookmarking pages and recommending other pages. Grow may set cookies or use your browser’s local storage, and it collects information about your visit and device to run those features and to report audience statistics. If you choose to sign up for a Grow feature that asks for your email address, that information is collected by Grow under Mediavine’s privacy policy, not by us. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings or use a content blocker; practice and puzzles keep working if you do.
  • We do not run advertising scripts today.
  • Our host, Cloudflare, keeps standard server logs (such as IP address and pages requested) for security and to keep the site running.
  • Cloudflare Web Analytics counts page views without cookies and without identifying you.
  • If we add ads later, we will update this page first and explain what they collect and how to opt out.

The Mint Puzzles iPhone app

  • The app collects no data. It has no analytics, no ads and no account, and the puzzles are built into the app, so they work offline.
  • Your results, streaks and reminder setting are stored only on your phone. Deleting the app deletes them.
  • The daily reminder is a notification scheduled on your phone. It is off until you turn it on, and nothing is sent to us.
  • If you sign in to Game Center, your streak and number of solved puzzles are sent to Apple for the leaderboards, under Apple’s privacy policy. We can’t see who you are.
  • Links to this website open in your browser, where the website section above applies.
