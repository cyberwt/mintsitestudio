How GRE scoring works

Your GRE score report has three numbers: Verbal Reasoning, Quantitative Reasoning and Analytical Writing.

MeasureScore scale
Verbal Reasoning130–170, in 1-point increments
Quantitative Reasoning130–170, in 1-point increments
Analytical Writing0–6, in half-point increments

How Verbal and Quant are calculated

For each measure, your score starts from the number of questions you answered correctly across both sections. Because the second section is adaptive, ETS then adjusts for the difficulty of the questions you saw, so the same number of correct answers can be worth more after a harder second section.

Never leave a question blank. There is no penalty for a wrong answer, so a guess can only help. If you’re running out of time, mark a guess on every remaining question.

Multi-part questions (select all that apply, two- and three-blank text completion, sentence equivalence) earn credit only when every part is right. There is no partial credit, and our practice questions are graded the same way.

Analytical Writing

The Issue essay is scored on a 0–6 scale by a trained reader and by ETS’s scoring engine. Strong essays take a clear position, support it with specific reasons and examples, and stay organized. Polish matters less than clear reasoning.

When scores arrive and how long they last

  • You see unofficial Verbal and Quant scores on screen at the end of the test.
  • Official scores, including Analytical Writing, are usually available about 8–10 days after test day.
  • Scores can be reported to schools for 5 years after your test date.

Retakes and ScoreSelect

You can take the GRE General Test once every 21 days, up to five times in any rolling 12-month period. With ScoreSelect you choose which test dates’ scores to send to schools.

What is a good GRE score?

It depends on the programs you’re applying to. Look up the median or typical scores your target programs publish, and aim a few points above them. ETS publishes percentile tables that show how each score compares with recent test takers.

Source: ETS’s GRE scores and registration pages, checked November 2026. Policies can change; confirm on ets.org.
