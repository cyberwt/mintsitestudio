GRE math formulas: the Quant cheat sheet

GRE Quant is high-school math tested cleverly. These are the rules that come up again and again. No formula sheet is provided on test day, so know them cold.

Arithmetic and number properties

  • Percent change = (new − old) ÷ old × 100%.
  • A rise of p% then a fall of p% leaves you below where you started: (1 + p)(1 − p) = 1 − p².
  • Compound interest: A = P(1 + r)t. Simple interest: I = Prt.
  • odd × odd = odd; anything × even = even; odd ± odd = even; odd ± even = odd.
  • Number of divisors: write n = paqb…, then the count is (a + 1)(b + 1)…
  • Divisible by 3 (or 9) when the digit sum is; by 4 when the last two digits are.

Exponents and roots

  • xa · xb = xa+b; xa ÷ xb = xa−b; (xa)b = xab; x0 = 1; x−a = 1/xa.
  • xn + xn = 2xn, not x2n. For example, 210 + 210 = 211.
  • For 0 < x < 1: x³ < x² < x < √x.
  • If x² = k (k > 0), then x = ±√k. Don’t forget the negative root.

Algebra

  • (a + b)² = a² + 2ab + b²; (a − b)² = a² − 2ab + b²; a² − b² = (a + b)(a − b).
  • |x − c| < d means c − d < x < c + d.
  • Quadratic formula: x = (−b ± √(b² − 4ac)) ÷ 2a.
  • Rate × time = distance (or work). Average speed = total distance ÷ total time.
  • Working together: 1/T = 1/A + 1/B.

Geometry

  • Triangle angles sum to 180°. Polygon interior angles sum to (n − 2) × 180°.
  • Area of a triangle = ½ × base × height. Pythagorean theorem: a² + b² = c². Common triples: 3-4-5, 5-12-13, 8-15-17.
  • 45-45-90 triangle sides: x, x, x√2. 30-60-90 triangle sides: x, x√3, 2x.
  • Circle: area πr², circumference 2πr. An arc or sector is (central angle ÷ 360) of the whole.
  • Square with diagonal d has area d²/2. Rectangular box volume = lwh; cylinder volume = πr²h.
  • Third side of a triangle is between the difference and the sum of the other two sides.

Coordinate geometry

  • Slope = (y₂ − y₁) ÷ (x₂ − x₁). Line: y = mx + b.
  • Parallel lines have equal slopes; perpendicular slopes multiply to −1.
  • Distance = √((x₂ − x₁)² + (y₂ − y₁)²).

Statistics

  • Mean = sum ÷ count, so sum = mean × count.
  • Median: middle value (average of the two middle values for an even count). Mode: most frequent. Range: max − min.
  • Standard deviation measures spread around the mean. Adding a constant to every value doesn’t change it; multiplying every value by k multiplies it by |k|.
  • In a normal distribution, about 68% of values are within 1 SD of the mean and about 95% within 2 SD.

Counting and probability

  • Arrangements of n items: n!. Ordered picks of k from n: n! ÷ (n − k)!. Unordered (combinations): n! ÷ (k!(n − k)!).
  • P(A or B) = P(A) + P(B) − P(A and B). Independent events: P(A and B) = P(A) × P(B).
  • “Without replacement” means the second probability uses one fewer item.
  • Inclusion–exclusion: |A or B| = |A| + |B| − |both|.
