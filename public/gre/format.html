GRE test format: sections, questions and timing

The GRE General Test was shortened in September 2023. It now takes about 1 hour and 58 minutes and has five sections.

SectionQuestionsTime
Analytical Writing: Analyze an Issue1 essay task30 min
Verbal Reasoning, section 11218 min
Verbal Reasoning, section 21523 min
Quantitative Reasoning, section 11221 min
Quantitative Reasoning, section 21526 min
Total54 questions + 1 essay1 hr 58 min

Analytical Writing always comes first. The Verbal and Quant sections follow, and their order can vary. The test is offered at test centers and at home.

The adaptive second section

Verbal and Quant are section-level adaptive: how well you do on the first section of a measure decides how hard the second one is. Within a section, every question counts the same, and you can skip, mark and come back to questions or change answers before time runs out.

Verbal Reasoning question types

Reading comprehension

Passages from one paragraph to several, from the sciences, humanities and everyday topics. Most questions have five choices and one answer; some ask you to select every correct answer from three choices; some ask you to pick a sentence in the passage.

Text completion

A passage of one to five sentences with one, two or three blanks. One-blank questions have five choices; two- and three-blank questions have three choices per blank. You only get credit if every blank is right.

Sentence equivalence

One sentence, one blank, six choices. Pick the two words that each complete the sentence and give it the same meaning. Both must be right for credit.

Quantitative Reasoning question types

Quantitative comparison

Compare Quantity A with Quantity B and choose: A is greater, B is greater, they are equal, or the relationship cannot be determined.

Multiple choice: one answer

Five choices, one correct answer.

Multiple choice: one or more answers

Select every correct choice. The question may or may not tell you how many to pick. No partial credit.

Numeric entry

Type an integer, a decimal or a fraction. Fractions don’t have to be reduced.

Some Quant questions come in data interpretation sets that share a table or graph. An on-screen calculator is available for the Quant sections.

What Quant covers

Arithmetic, algebra, geometry and data analysis at roughly a high-school level. Our GRE math formula sheet lists the rules that come up most.
