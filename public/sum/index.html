    How to play

    • Tap a number, tap an operation, then tap a second number. The two numbers are replaced by the result.
    • Reach the target exactly to clear the round. You don't have to use every number.
    • Only whole numbers are allowed: subtraction can't go below zero and division must come out even.
    • Each day has three rounds: Easy, Medium and Hard. Every target has at least one exact solution.
