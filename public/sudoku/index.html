Mini Sudoku

How to play

  • Fill the grid so every row, every column and every 2×3 box has the numbers 1 to 6 exactly once.
  • Tap a square, then tap a number. On a keyboard, use the arrow keys and type 1 to 6.
  • Each puzzle has exactly one solution, and you can always find the next number by logic.
