Mint Word

How to play

  • Guess the hidden five-letter word in six tries. Each guess must be a real word.
  • After each guess the tiles change color: teal means the letter is in the right spot, orange means it is in the word but in another spot, and grey means it is not in the word.
  • Letters can appear more than once. Type on your keyboard or tap the keys below the grid.
