Mint Box

Use all 12 letters. Try to do it in 5 words or fewer.

How to play

  • Make words using the letters around the square. Each word needs at least three letters.
  • Letters next to each other in a word must come from different sides of the square. Letters can be reused.
  • Each new word starts with the last letter of the word before it.
  • Use all twelve letters to solve the day. Fewer words is better: there is always a way to do it in two.
