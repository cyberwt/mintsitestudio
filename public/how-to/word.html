Best starting words for a five-letter word game

In Mint Word you have six tries to find a hidden five-letter word. Teal means the right letter in the right spot, orange means the letter is in the word somewhere else. A strong first guess turns up two or three letters almost every time. We counted the letters in every answer in Mint Word’s list to find the best openers.

Which letters show up most

S is lower than in most English word lists because Mint Word answers are never plain plurals (no “cats”, “boxes”) and never end in -ed. Q, Z, X and J are rare: each turns up in only 7 to 12 of the 871 answers.

The best first guesses

Ranked by how many answers share letters with them, among everyday words: STARE, ARISE, IRATE, AROSE and TRACE. Each one shares at least one letter with 92–95% of answers, and lands a teal tile (a letter in the right spot) on 45–48% of them.

A strong two-word opening

If you like to play two set words before you start thinking, pick a pair with ten different letters. AROSE then UNTIL shares two or more letters with 99% of the answers. STARE then CLOUD, or TRACE then SOLID, are close behind at 95–97%.

Strategy after the first guess

  • Use what you learn. Keep teal letters in place and move orange ones to a new spot.
  • Spend a guess to test letters. If you have four teal letters and many possible fifth letters (like _IGHT: light, might, night, sight, tight), a guess that tests several of those letters at once beats guessing them one by one.
  • Remember doubles. Letters can repeat (“hello”, “sheep”). If you are stuck on four letters, try a repeat.
  • Grey letters are clues too. The keyboard below the grid greys out letters you’ve ruled out.
