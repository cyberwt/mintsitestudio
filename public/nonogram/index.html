Mint Nonogram

How to play

  • The numbers next to each row and above each column give the lengths of the filled runs in that line, in order.
  • There is at least one empty square between two runs. "3 1" means three filled, a gap, then one filled.
  • Tap or drag to fill squares. Switch to ✕ to mark squares you know are empty; marks are only notes.
  • A clue fades when its line matches. Every puzzle can be solved by logic alone, one line at a time.
